## Followups
- **`mergePdf` crashes cryptically on an out-of-bounds `originalIndex` into a
  *present* source.** The loop guards a missing source document (`!sourcePdf` →
  helpful "Source document missing for page N") but not a valid source with a

  message. Behavior change → left for a human per the run's fence.
